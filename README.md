# TendieStudio

**Make it. Animate it. Keep it.**

TendieStudio is an upcoming native iPhone app for creating and sharing animated wallpapers. Built with the sideloading community in mind, it's designed to put the creative tools and the finished wallpapers in your hands, not behind an account or a subscription.

> **Status: early development.** TendieStudio is not yet available to install. There is no working IPA or public release at this stage. The features below describe the direction of the project, not completed functionality.

## What is TendieStudio?

An on-device wallpaper studio with three connected spaces:

### Discover
Find animated wallpapers made by other people, preview them and save your favourites.

We plan to explore compatibility with the existing CAPlayground community collection. Whether direct browsing or publishing is possible will depend on its available interfaces and the maintainers' rules. TendieStudio is not affiliated with CAPlayground.

### Studio
Turn your own images and artwork into moving wallpapers.

The planned editor includes:

- Import images and add multiple layers.
- Move, resize and rotate objects directly on a canvas.
- Animate with keyframes, loops, easing and playful motion effects.
- Preview animations at iPhone wallpaper dimensions.
- Save a project, come back later and keep editing it.

The goal is an editor that feels natural on a touchscreen, not a desktop video editor squeezed onto a phone.

### Library
Keep everything together, on your device.

- Your original creations and unfinished drafts.
- Wallpapers you've imported or downloaded.
- Editable project files and local backups or exports.

Creating and editing shouldn't depend on a connection or an account.

## Exporting and sharing

TendieStudio aims to support wallpaper formats used by the sideloading community, including exploring `.tendies` compatibility. **Format support isn't implemented or verified yet.** We'll only advertise an export option once real output has been tested with compatible wallpaper tools.

Community sharing is planned as an optional feature. Your locally saved projects should remain yours whether you publish them or not.

## Built for freedom

- **Native iOS experience:** Designed for Swift and SwiftUI, with fluid touch interactions.
- **Local first:** Editing and personal projects should work offline.
- **No forced account:** Community features may need connectivity, but creating shouldn't.
- **No subscription requirement:** Core editing is intended to be freely available.
- **Sideloading friendly:** Distribution is planned for users who install apps outside the App Store.

Sideloading does **not** automatically remove iOS restrictions. Wallpaper application methods, permissions, signing requirements and device compatibility will be documented and tested as the app develops.

## Availability

**There is no IPA to download yet.** Supported iOS versions, build instructions and installation steps will be added when there's a working, tested application.

You can follow this repository for development and future releases.
